Transforming Education in South Africa

LearnTrack is an innovative solution that bridges critical gaps in higher education through a student-centric ecosystem combining gamified learning, peer-to-peer knowledge monetization, and blockchain-powered certification.

Systemic Challenges in South African Education

Declining Engagement

Students struggle with decreasing motivation and engagement in digital learning environments.

Financial Pressures

High costs of study materials create significant barriers to quality education access.

Employment Skills Gap

Graduates lack recognized skills that employers value in the competitive job market.

The LearnTrack Solution

The LearnTrack Platform emerges as an innovative solution, bridging these gaps through a student-centric ecosystem that combines:

Gamified learning to increase engagement and motivation, peer-to-peer knowledge monetization to alleviate financial pressures, and blockchain-powered certification to provide trusted, verifiable credentials.

Developed initially for the University of Mpumalanga (UoM), LearnTrack addresses local realities while creating a scalable model for South African higher education.
